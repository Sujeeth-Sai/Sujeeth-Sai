# Blur WhatsApp Chats Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Blur WhatsApp Chats changes the visual display of WhatsApp Web. It blurs selected parts of the page, can hide selected chats, and can cover the page during a screen share. It operates in your browser.

## Information the extension uses

To apply visual privacy controls, the extension examines WhatsApp Web page elements such as contact and group names, chat previews, messages, and media. It does not save message text or media. If you choose to always blur or hide a chat, the extension saves that chat's display title in Chrome local storage so the rule can be applied later. A title may contain a person's name or other personal information.

The extension saves your visual preferences and limited usage counters in Chrome Sync storage. If you use Chrome Sync, Google may synchronize those settings across your signed-in browsers. The extension saves a random salt and PBKDF2 hash of an optional PIN in Chrome local storage. It does not save the PIN itself. A number entered into Quick chat is used to open a WhatsApp Web URL and is not saved by the extension. WhatsApp handles that page and its normal network activity.

## Sharing and transmission

Blur WhatsApp Chats does not send page content, chat titles, messages, media, PINs, phone numbers, or settings to its developer or to any analytics or advertising service. It has no developer-operated server or background network requests. Chrome Sync may synchronize the settings described above through Google if you use that browser feature. A Quick chat action opens a WhatsApp Web URL that includes the number you entered, at your request.

## Retention and control

Always-blurred and hidden chat titles stay in Chrome local storage until you remove the rule in Options or uninstall the extension. You can clear the optional PIN in Options. Settings and usage counters remain in browser storage until you change them or uninstall the extension, subject to Chrome's own Sync behavior.

## Limits

Blur WhatsApp Chats changes what is visible in the WhatsApp Web tab. It does not hide desktop notifications or other apps. Its PIN cover is a visual screen cover, not encryption. It cannot detect every meeting or screen share outside WhatsApp Web.

Blur WhatsApp Chats is independent of WhatsApp and Meta and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by them.
